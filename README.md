# Jarvis — demo

**Live demo:** https://pablogzzr.github.io/jarvis-demo/

Jarvis is Pablo González's personal AI chief of staff. Every night about fifty agents read his mail,
meetings, calendar, tasks and notes. Every morning this page shows him only what needs his judgment:
the day already briefed, meeting preps, follow-ups to approve, a lesson, coach notes on how he
communicated, and the state of every agent.

This is a public copy of the real panel with **an invented week**. Every person, client, task and note
in it is made up. Nothing you click is sent anywhere. Decisions you make live in your browser tab until
you press **Reset**.

What to try:
- **Start the run**: approve (✓) or drop (✕) what the agents drafted overnight.
- **See your agents**: the agents grouped by the goal they serve, each with a trust score.
- **Your brain**: a 3D map of the notes in the second brain.
- **Study** and **Me**: the study queue, and where the week's time actually went.
- **⌘K** opens every command. **?** shows the keyboard shortcuts.
